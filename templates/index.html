    Process Overview

    How AI Resume Analyzer Works

    Three straightforward steps to help you align your resume with job requirements.

    1. Upload Resume PDF

    Upload your resume in PDF format. PyPDF2 parses the document and extracts clean text without altering your file.

    2. Paste Job Description

    Input the target job responsibilities. The analyzer extracts required skills and compares them against your resume.

    3. Save & Track Results

    Review your score, gap recommendations, and track all past analyses securely in your MySQL database history.

    About The Project

    About AI Resume Analyzer

    AI Resume Analyzer is a full-stack web application developed with Python, Flask, Bootstrap, JavaScript, PyPDF2 and MySQL. It helps job seekers understand keyword matching algorithms, uncover hidden skill gaps, and track improvement over time.

    Security & Privacy: Database credentials are strictly managed using environment variables (.env) and never hardcoded or exposed in frontend code.
